# PREXON

![PREXON — connected real estate workflows](../brand/exports/prexon-linkedin.png)

**Ongoing independent software project · January 2026–present**

## Problem

Property, contract, payment and approval records belong to connected business workflows. A usable platform needs to keep those relationships understandable while controlling access and retaining an audit history.

## Contribution

My work spans product discovery, requirements definition, stakeholder coordination and delivery planning, translating business needs into practical platform capabilities.

## Delivered

Delivered a real estate and contract management platform covering properties, contracts, payments and approvals, with role-based access and audit trails.

![Connected implemented platform capabilities](../brand/exports/prexon-evidence.png)

The visual groups delivered capabilities; it does not represent a client's records or prescribe a single approval sequence.

## Current status

PREXON remains an independent project alongside my Information Systems degree. Source code and client records remain private.
